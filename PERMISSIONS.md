# Permission records

## I2VIS (T. Gerya)

On 10 November 2026, Taras Gerya (I2VIS author) replied by email:
"Yes, feel free to publish the patches." This was in response to a request
that described the patch files, the case configurations, and the build
instructions included in `solver_patch/`.

The permission covers:

- the patch files in `solver_patch/patch/`;
- the case configurations in `solver_patch/config/` (`init.t3c`,
  `mode.t3c`, `Makefile`);
- the build and application instructions in `solver_patch/`.

It does not cover the upstream I2VIS source, which is not redistributed
here; users must obtain it from the OSF record
(<https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/BNVTH>) and comply with its terms. The
patches and configurations remain outside the GeoSim MIT license.

The permission email is retained by the GeoSim authors.
